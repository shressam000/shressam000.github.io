02 / Accessibility & interaction design · 2025

City Watch

City Watch is a civic reporting app concept that gives people a straightforward way to report neighborhood issues and follow updates on their reports.

✦✧
UW ProtothonAccessible design / Figma

A simpler way to report an issue.

The concept connects community members with a simpler way to bring local issues to their city’s attention. For the UW Protothon, I reworked the reporting flow from five steps to three, using clearer categories, larger tap targets, and more readable updates. These choices make the experience easier to navigate for a wider range of people, including older adults.

03 / Research & information architecture · 2026

ZARA redesign

A team redesign proposing clearer navigation and product discovery for ZARA’s website.

✦✧
INFO 331 · Team projectResearch / Figma / Wireframes

Helping shoppers find what they need.

Our team proposed broader clothing categories, a top navigation bar for account and shopping controls, and a product grid that displays more items at once. We also recommended more precise search results and additional filters.

Our proposed redesign focused mainly on the women’s section of the U.S. website, and our testing sample was limited. I worked with Emily Wang Santamaria, Megan Pham, Mia Nakano, and Sunghee Park.
